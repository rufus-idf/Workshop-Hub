Introduction & Daily Start

This booklet is for workshop operators and fit-out teams. It explains how to use Workshop Hub from first login through to dispatch, using only front-end actions you perform in the app.

Start of shift routine
  1. On the login screen, tap your name to identify yourself for this session.
  2. Confirm the top-right badge is Online before editing anything.
  3. Press ↻ to refresh and pull latest job, stock and tracking updates.
  4. Check the Orders button for waiting imports (red indicator).
  5. Open Active jobs and identify today’s highest-priority projects.
Tip: If multiple staff are working at once, refresh before major updates (start/finish, deliveries, room moves).
Page 2 of 12

User Sessions & Login

Workshop Hub uses a simple name-picker instead of individual Google accounts. Everyone shares the workshop tablet/browser but each person identifies themselves at the start of their session so all activity is logged correctly.

Logging in
  1. When the app loads you will see the Who are you? screen.
  2. Tap your name from the list.
  3. You are taken straight to the jobs view — your name appears in the top-right header as 👤 Name.
What your session affects
  • All stock changes, panel updates, component packing, and delivery actions are logged under your name in the Tracking tab.
  • The Action List filter defaults to your name so you see your own tasks immediately.
  • New tasks you create pre-fill the Assigned By field with your name.
Signing out

Tap the ↩ icon (next to your name badge in the header) and confirm. The app returns to the login screen ready for the next person.

Important: Always sign out at the end of your session so the next person's actions are not logged under your name.

Job Flow & Screen Guide

Home tabs
  • Client Jobs: live customer work in progress.
  • Stock Builds: internal manufacturing jobs such as Workshop Stock.
  • Completed: closed jobs for reference.
Project drilldown

Tap a job card to open Operations, then choose one of three paths:

  • Manufacturing for panel/component production updates.
  • Delivery & Fitting for bucket/room allocation and site status.
  • CNC Station for loading sheet usage and stock deductions.

Use breadcrumbs at the top to jump back without losing orientation.

Manufacturing Workflow (Panels & Components)

Panels
  • Use search to find panel rows quickly by name/SKU.
  • Update each stage as work is completed: Cut → CNC'd → Edge Finished → Packed.
  • Packed quantity drives readiness for downstream delivery workflows.
Components
  • Mark hardware and fittings as packed as they are prepared.
  • If panel sets are complete but components are short, manufactured units are still limited.
Important: Update quantities immediately after a station completes work. Delayed updates create false readiness in other areas.
Page 5 of 12

Damaged Panels Procedure

Use the panel damage workflow whenever material is unusable due to chips, scratches, incorrect machining, transport damage, or handling errors.

How to record damage
  1. Open the affected panel row.
  2. Tap the damage action button.
  3. Enter Quantity Damaged and a clear Reason.
  4. Submit to log the issue and keep counts accurate.
Best-practice reason notes
  • Good: “Front edge chipped during edging pass; 1pc re-cut required.”
  • Poor: “Damaged.” (too vague for follow-up decisions)

Always record damage before end-of-shift handover so replacements can be scheduled correctly.

Delivery, Bucket & Rooms

Bucket tab

Bucket is for packed items not yet assigned to a room. Use it as your staging area before loading or room planning.

Rooms tab
  • Create/select room names and move items from bucket into the correct room.
  • Use consistent room naming (e.g. “Room 101”, “Kitchen”, “Reception”).
  • Export room lists when you need install paperwork.
Status updates
  • Delivered = physically on site.
  • Fitted = installed and complete.
Page 7 of 12

Workshop Stock Jobs (Internal Builds)

Workshop Stock jobs are internal builds, not direct customer installations. They typically appear in the stock-focused area of the home screen.

How to run stock jobs properly
  • Treat them like normal manufacturing jobs for accuracy: cut/process/edge/pack updates still matter.
  • Keep descriptions clear so other staff can identify stock items quickly.
  • When stock is consumed, ensure inventory and assignments reflect where it went.
Why this matters

Poorly tracked stock builds cause false availability and wasted production time when jobs rely on items that were never truly ready.

Page 8 of 12

CNC Station & Stock Deductions

  • Use CNC view to log material usage against Full Sheets and Offcuts.
  • Deduct sheets as the cut is performed, not later.
  • If you switch material mid-run, update selection immediately to keep stock valuation accurate.

Correct CNC deductions keep procurement, planning, and shop-floor decisions reliable.

Page 9 of 12

Stock Lists & Inventory

Stock list sections
  • Wood: sub-tabs for In Stock, On Order, and Offcuts.
  • Components: sub-tabs for In Stock and On Order.
  • Edge Band: roll stock and On Order.
  • Furniture: finished stock items.
Adjusting stock levels

Use the + / − buttons on any stock row to adjust quantities. You will be prompted for a reason — every change is logged in the Tracking tab against your name.

On Order sub-tabs

When you have placed a supplier order, use the On Order sub-tab to record how many are expected. When the delivery arrives, press Receive to move quantity from On Order into In Stock automatically.

Adding new stock items

To add a brand-new material or component that doesn't exist in the list yet, tap the + Add button above the search bar for that stock type. A form will appear:

  • Wood: Material, Colour Code (free text), Size (dropdown or custom), Price. Current stock and On Order are optional.
  • Components: Name, Item Code, Category (dropdown or add new), Supplier (dropdown or add new), Price. Stock, On Order and Supplier Link are optional.
  • Edge Band: Material, Thickness (dropdown or custom), Roll Length (dropdown or custom), Price. Stock and On Order are optional.

Once saved, the new item appears in the list immediately and is written to the background spreadsheet.

Tip: Use search bars to verify availability before committing a rush cut or replacement. When parts are damaged, check stock lists straight away to avoid line stoppages.

Action List

The Action List is a shared task board for directors and workshop managers to assign jobs to team members. It is accessed from the Action List tab in the main navigation.

Viewing tasks
  • When you open the Action List it automatically filters to your name so you see your own tasks first.
  • Use the filter dropdown at the top to switch to another team member's view, or select All Team Members to see everything.
  • Tasks are sorted by priority (High first) and then by status (Not Started before In Progress).
  • Overdue tasks are highlighted in red.
Task priorities & statuses
  • Priority: High (red badge), Medium (amber), Low (green).
  • Status: Not Started → In Progress → Completed.
Starting and completing tasks
  1. Tap ▶ Start on a task to mark it In Progress — this records progress without removing it from the list.
  2. Tap ✓ Complete to mark it done. Completed tasks disappear from the list view but remain on the background spreadsheet for reference.
Creating a new task
  1. Tap + New Task at the top of the Action List.
  2. Fill in: Task description, Assign To (who should do it), Assigned By (pre-filled with your name), Priority, and optionally a Due Date.
  3. Press Save Task — it appears immediately in the list.
Tip: Always set a due date on urgent tasks so they highlight as overdue if not actioned in time.

Orders Import & Tracking Audits

Orders
  1. Open Orders when a waiting badge is shown.
  2. Review pending Shopify rows and import required jobs.
  3. Check imported jobs appear in the expected home tab.
Tracking
  • Filter by user, order ID, and source when investigating changes.
  • Sort newest/oldest depending on whether you are debugging a recent issue or auditing a full timeline.

End-of-Day Checklist

  1. Finish all pending quantity updates (panels/components/delivery).
  2. Record all damaged panels with quantity + clear reason.
  3. Confirm bucket/room allocations match physical staging.
  4. Verify delivered/fitted statuses for site teams.
  5. Check the Action List — mark any tasks you completed today as done.
  6. Refresh once and resolve any obvious mismatches before handover.
  7. Tap ↩ in the header and sign out so the next person's session is logged correctly.

Outcome: The next shift starts with a trustworthy system state and no hidden surprises.
